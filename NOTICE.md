# Third-party notices

## Word lists

The word lists in `js/words.js` are derived from:

- **SCOWL** (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), levels 10 to 70, as packaged by the
  `wordlist-english` npm package. Copyright 2000-2016 by Kevin Atkinson and the other copyright
  holders named in its notice. SCOWL's full copyright and permission notice, which also covers the
  WordNet, VarCon and 12Dicts material it includes, is reproduced in
  [licenses/SCOWL.txt](licenses/SCOWL.txt).
- **ENABLE** (Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon), released into the public domain by its
  authors. Thanks to them for the list.

Answer words were selected with the help of frequency data from
[wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer. That data was only used to choose
words and is not redistributed here.

## Definitions

Definitions shown after a game are fetched at runtime from the
[Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev/), which serves content from
[Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) under the
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) license. Each definition links
back to its Wiktionary entry.
