Wordle

Game mode

How to play

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
  • The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Examples

CRAFT

C is in the word and in the correct spot.

PILOT

L is in the word but in the wrong spot.

MOUSE

S is not in the word in any spot.

Two ways to play

  • Daily gives everyone the same word. A new puzzle arrives at midnight.
  • Unlimited serves a fresh random word whenever you like.

Type on your keyboard or tap the keys. For an extra challenge, turn on Hard Mode in Settings.
